Our house rules

Your version: J blind swaps, Q looks then swaps. Standard 52-card deck, no Jokers. Lowest total wins. Four face-down cards each. Look at your bottom two at the start, then hide them.

Peek at ONE card anywhere. Hide it, then optionally swap ANY one of yours with ANY one of theirs. The peeked and swapped cards can be different. No second peek.

On your turn draw unseen, then replace one of your cards or discard your draw. Powers activate only when you discard a fresh draw. A card taken from the discard pile must replace one of yours.

After every discard, both players can match its rank using a face-down card on either side. Match yours: remove it. Match theirs: remove it, then give them one of yours face down. Wrong: keep the chosen card and take one unseen penalty card yourself. Face-card matches must be the same rank, not just the same points. Powers never trigger from matches. Tap Continue when done. Both players must continue before the next turn. No speed advantage or timers.

Call Cambio instead of drawing. Your hand locks: no more peeking, swaps or matches involving your cards. Your opponent gets one final turn, then reveal and score. No extra penalty for calling. Emptying your hand ends the round immediately.

Choose any positive whole-number points target or number of rounds. Scores carry across rounds, red Kings included. When someone reaches the target, the lowest total wins. A tie stays a tie. Starting player alternates each round.

If the draw pile runs out, shuffle the discards except the top card. If no cards remain to draw, score the round.
